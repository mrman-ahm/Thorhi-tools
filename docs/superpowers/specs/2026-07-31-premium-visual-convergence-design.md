# THROHI Premium Visual Convergence Design

## Objective

Unify every `/rebuild` route into one premium, regal, trustworthy visual system without weakening catalogue usability, inquiry conversion, accessibility, truthful content boundaries, or responsive behavior.

The result must feel like an established surgical-instrument manufacturer rather than a generic ecommerce template, design-agency portfolio, dashboard, or AI-generated landing page.

## Approved direction: Regal Technical Corporate

The visual language combines:

- restrained editorial serif typography for major headings;
- clear sans-serif typography for body copy and controls;
- monospaced typography only for catalogue codes, status, and technical metadata;
- deep ink and navy surfaces;
- warm ivory paper surfaces rather than flat white;
- muted brass accents for prestige;
- surgical green for actionable and verified states;
- steel-blue structure for technical hierarchy;
- real instrument photography as the dominant visual material.

The site must remain calm, medically credible, and procurement-focused. It must not imitate fashion luxury, hospitality, gaming, or speculative technology branding.

## Typography

### Display type

Use Cormorant Garamond for primary page titles, major section headings, and selected product names. It provides a regal editorial character while remaining appropriate for an established manufacturer when used with disciplined spacing and weight.

### Interface and body type

Use Instrument Sans for navigation, buttons, forms, body copy, and supporting descriptions. Archivo remains available as a fallback and for legacy surfaces during convergence.

### Technical type

Use IBM Plex Mono for catalogue codes, verified/pending states, eyebrow labels, source status, and concise metadata.

### Size rules

- Important content must never be hidden in microtype.
- Body copy should normally render at 16–19 px with generous line height.
- Form labels and navigation must not fall below 13 px.
- Technical metadata may reach 12 px only when secondary and high-contrast.
- Major page titles should scale fluidly but avoid clipping or dominating the entire viewport.
- Serif display typography is reserved for hierarchy, not used for dense controls or long paragraphs.

## Color and material system

Core palette:

- Ink: `#06131d`
- Deep navy: `#0a2233`
- Royal navy: `#12384f`
- Warm ivory: `#f7f3ea`
- Paper: `#fbfaf6`
- Steel: `#d9e3e7`
- Text: `#152b36`
- Muted text: `#586b73`
- Surgical green: `#236b4b`
- Light green: `#a7d8bd`
- Muted brass: `#ad8950`
- Light brass: `#d8bd84`
- Error red: `#9b3e42`

Brass is used sparingly for premium rules, selected decorative details, and important editorial markers. Green remains the principal action and verified-state color.

Surfaces should use subtle tonal gradients, fine rules, and restrained shadow depth. Large stacks of rounded cards are prohibited.

## Layout system

### Global shell

- Increase the maximum visual container to support premium wide compositions while preserving readable text measures.
- Use an 80 px desktop header and 66 px mobile header.
- Establish consistent vertical rhythm with larger section spacing and fewer arbitrary blocks.
- Use a fine top accent rule and disciplined border hierarchy.
- Preserve skip links, focus states, fixed navigation, and no-index rebuild behavior.

### Header

- Treat the logo as a refined manufacturer mark with more breathing room.
- Navigation uses confident medium-size text, not tiny uppercase labels.
- Inquiry List becomes the principal header utility with a clear count badge.
- Product and search panels use warm paper/ink contrast and large readable product-family links.
- Mobile navigation uses a full-height editorial menu with clear route groups and a strong inquiry action.

### Footer

- Create a stronger closing composition with a serif brand statement, clear route columns, verified origin, and restrained operational status.
- Avoid filler slogans and social placeholders.
- Keep only real navigation and verified company information.

## Page convergence

### Homepage

- Preserve the cinematic opening and real instrument media.
- Strengthen the normal hero with serif hierarchy, more deliberate negative space, and a premium search/inquiry composition.
- Convert division and selected-family areas from generic records into an editorial procurement index.
- Maintain concise, truthful content.
- Use instrument imagery as visual anchors rather than decorative background noise.

### Catalogue

- Retain the Precision Catalogue Ledger architecture.
- Upgrade masthead typography and specimen presentation.
- Increase important control text and improve filter readability.
- Refine product records with warmer surfaces, brass/steel rules, and clearer title/code relationships.
- Do not reduce scanning density or URL-backed behavior.

### Product detail

- Turn the examination stage into a premium archival presentation.
- Use serif product names with restrained technical metadata.
- Preserve base/variant identities, return context, and inquiry state.
- Make inquiry actions more prominent without ecommerce language.

### Inquiry List

- Preserve the procurement-desk structure.
- Improve form label size, field spacing, review hierarchy, and trust cues.
- Maintain durable-backend, attachment, validation, and Turnstile behavior.
- Avoid decorative text that does not help submission.

### Company, Catalogues, Contact, and history

- Use one shared editorial hero language.
- Make titles, summaries, ledgers, and truth boundaries feel authored as one system.
- Keep factual boundaries explicit but visually integrated rather than apologetic.

### Success, empty, error, and pending states

- Use the same serif/sans hierarchy and premium spacing.
- State what happened, what remains, and the next action clearly.
- Do not add celebratory filler or unsupported promises.

## Motion

- Use motion only for hierarchy, panel transitions, image examination, and navigation state.
- Keep durations restrained and easing controlled.
- No universal entrance animation, floating decoration, parallax overload, or continuous background motion.
- Preserve complete reduced-motion parity.

## Accessibility and trust

- WCAG AA color contrast remains a blocking requirement.
- Important information cannot rely on brass or green color alone.
- Controls retain 44 px minimum target size.
- Text remains readable at 320 px without horizontal overflow.
- Focus indicators remain visible on every surface.
- No invented certifications, materials, export claims, history, contact channels, or catalogue files.

## Implementation boundaries

This milestone changes presentation and shared visual primitives. It does not:

- publish the rebuild;
- merge into the public site;
- invent missing client content;
- configure real Cloudflare resource IDs or secrets;
- redesign catalogue data structures;
- change inquiry business rules;
- add pricing, checkout, or cart language.

## Acceptance criteria

- Every `/rebuild` route uses the same display, body, technical, color, spacing, border, and focus systems.
- Major headings use the approved serif display face.
- Important body, navigation, form, and action text is never unreasonably small.
- Header and footer feel premium and trustworthy on desktop and mobile.
- Homepage, catalogue, product, inquiry, and corporate routes retain their existing functional behavior.
- No horizontal overflow at 320, 390, 768, 1280, or 1440 px.
- Reduced-motion mode remains complete.
- No serious or critical accessibility violations are introduced.
- No filler copy or unverified business claims are added.
